\# City Wise IPL Match Analysis



\## About

In this analysis, I used Google Sheets to find out which city has hosted the most IPL matches in my dataset.



\## What I Did

\- Checked the city column in the IPL dataset.

\- Created a Pivot Table to count matches city-wise.

\- Sorted the counts from highest to lowest.

\- Created a bar chart to compare the cities.



\## Results



| City | Number of Matches |

|---|---:|

| Mumbai | 77 |

| Bangalore | 58 |

| Kolkata | 54 |

| Delhi | 53 |

| Chennai | 48 |



\## My Observations

\- Mumbai has the highest number of matches, with 77.

\- Bangalore is in second place with 58 matches.

\- Kolkata comes third with 54 matches.

\- There are also 7 records where the city is shown as unknown.



\## Conclusion

From this analysis, I found that Mumbai has the highest number of matches in my dataset.
