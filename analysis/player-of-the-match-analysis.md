\# IPL Player of the Match Analysis



\## About

I analyzed the player\_of\_match column to find out which players received the most Player of the Match awards in my IPL dataset.



\## What I Did

\- Created a Pivot Table in Google Sheets.

\- Added player\_of\_match to Rows.

\- Used COUNTA to count the awards.

\- Sorted the counts from highest to lowest.

\- Compared the top players.



\## Results



| Player | Awards |

|---|---:|

| CH Gayle | 17 |

| YK Pathan | 16 |

| AB de Villiers | 15 |

| DA Warner | 14 |

| SK Raina | 13 |



\## My Observation

CH Gayle has the highest count with 17 awards. YK Pathan is second with 16, so the difference between them is only one award.



\## Conclusion

CH Gayle received the most Player of the Match awards in my dataset.
