\# IPL Toss Decision Analysis



\## About

I analyzed the toss decisions in the IPL dataset using Google Sheets to find out whether teams chose batting or fielding more often after winning the toss.



\## What I Did

\- Created a Pivot Table using the `toss\_decision` column.

\- Counted the number of batting and fielding decisions.

\- Created a pie chart to compare both decisions.



\## Results



| Toss Decision | Count | Percentage |

|---|---:|---:|

| Bat | 262 | 45.4% |

| Field | 315 | 54.6% |

| Total | 577 | 100% |



\## My Observation

Fielding was chosen more often than batting in this dataset. There were 53 more fielding decisions than batting decisions.



\## Conclusion

The analysis shows that fielding was the more common toss decision in the matches included in this dataset.
