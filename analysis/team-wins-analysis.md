\# IPL Team-wise Match Wins



\## About

I analyzed the winner column in my IPL dataset to find out which teams won the most matches.



\## What I Did

\- Created a Pivot Table in Google Sheets.

\- Added winner to Rows.

\- Used COUNTA of winner to count wins.

\- Sorted the results from highest to lowest.

\- Created a column chart.



\## Results



| Team | Matches Won |

|---|---:|

| Mumbai Indians | 80 |

| Chennai Super Kings | 79 |

| Royal Challengers Bangalore | 70 |

| Kolkata Knight Riders | 68 |

| Rajasthan Royals | 63 |



\## My Observation

Mumbai Indians have the highest count with 80 wins, followed closely by Chennai Super Kings with 79. The difference between them is only one match.



\## Data Quality Note

\## Conclusion

Mumbai Indians have the highest recorded number of match wins in my dataset.
