\# Toss Winner vs Match Winner Analysis



\## About

I analyzed the toss winner and match winner columns in my IPL dataset to check how often the same team won both the toss and the match.



\## What I Did

\- Created a Pivot Table using toss\_winner and winner.

\- Compared the toss-winning team with the match-winning team.

\- Created a summary table.

\- Made a pie chart to compare both results.



\## Results

\- Toss winner also won: 291 matches (50.7%)

\- Toss winner did not win: 283 matches (49.3%)

\- Total records considered: 574



\## My Observation

The toss-winning team also won the match in 291 records. The difference between the two outcomes is small.



\## Conclusion

In this dataset, the toss winner and match winner were the same in about 50.7% of records. This alone does not prove that winning the toss causes a team to win the match.
